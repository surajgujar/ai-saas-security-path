## 6. Relocation pipeline (⚠️ not legal advice; verify with an immigration adviser)

| Priority | Country | Main route | Key facts | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **UK** | Skilled Worker (job offer) · **Global Talent** in parallel | Skilled Worker general threshold £41,700. Global Talent needs recognition evidence; ILR in 3 yrs (Talent) | London: labs, banks, AISI. Apply for Global Talent once you have 2–3 talks and the OSS (~Q3) |
| 1 | **Ireland** | Critical Skills Employment Permit | EU HQs of Google, Microsoft, Meta, OpenAI | Strong for hyperscalers, fintech and pharma |
| 1 | **Germany** | EU Blue Card | €50,700 (€45,934 shortage/IT) for 2026 | Munich/Berlin/Frankfurt; banks, insurers, pharma |
| 2 | **Netherlands** | Highly Skilled Migrant | Amsterdam fintech and cloud | — |
| 2 | **UAE** | Employment visa → **Golden Visa** (skilled professional: AED 30,000/month basic salary + degree; direct nomination possible for cyber specialists) | Fast processing, no income tax | Good for a *Head* title; banks and government AI programmes |
| 2 | **Singapore** | Employment Pass + COMPASS (40 pts) | 2026 minimum S$5,600 (S$6,200 financial services), **rising with age, up to S$10,700 / S$11,800 for 45+**. Cybersecurity is on the shortage list (bonus points) | MAS-regulated FS is an ideal fit for your FS + AI profile |
| 3 | **Canada** | Express Entry (STEM category) / Global Talent Stream via employer | New 2026 categories favour Canadian experience; slower without a job offer | Treat as a 12–24-month option |

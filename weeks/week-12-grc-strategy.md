# Week 12 — GRC, Standards & Strategy (14 – 20 Dec)

**Why (JD):** "Contribute to the Siemens-wide AI and SaaS security strategy and roadmap"; "Develop AI and SaaS security standards … policies, audits, and regulatory alignment"
**Goal:** Pull 11 weeks into the artefacts a SME actually produces — and a strong capstone to show in interviews.

## Learn (2.5 h)
- [ ] **NIST AI RMF 1.0** + **Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1)** — Govern / Map / Measure / Manage
- [ ] **ISO/IEC 42001** (AI management system) — structure and Annex A controls at overview level; how it relates to ISO 27001
- [ ] **EU AI Act** — risk tiers, GPAI obligations, deployer vs provider duties, timeline. ⚠️ Check current status of application dates (the EU "Digital Omnibus" proposals may shift high-risk obligations)
- [ ] **EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** and **NIS2** — relevant to Siemens as a manufacturer of products with digital elements
- [ ] **CSA AI Controls Matrix (AICM)** and **Google SAIF** — control catalogues to borrow from
- [ ] Siemens context: Industrial Copilot / Xcelerator, Siemens ProductCERT, Siemens' public AI principles (see `interview-prep/siemens-research.md`)

## Lab (4 h) → `labs/12-standard-and-roadmap/`
- [ ] **AI & SaaS Security Standard** (8–10 pages): scope, roles, mandatory controls (reuse Weeks 6–11), exceptions process, mapping to NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001 / EU AI Act / OWASP
- [ ] **AI system intake & risk-assessment questionnaire** (for business teams)
- [ ] **12-month roadmap**: Q1 visibility (AI/SaaS inventory, Shadow AI) → Q2 guardrails & identity baseline → Q3 SSPM rollout, red-team program → Q4 audit readiness & metrics
- [ ] **Metrics/KPIs**: % AI apps assessed, ASR trend, % SaaS apps under SSPM, OAuth grants reviewed, mean time to deprovision, NHI with owner

## Interview questions
1. How would you build an AI security program at Siemens in your first year?
2. What does the EU AI Act require from a *deployer* of a third-party LLM?
3. How do you get product teams to adopt a standard instead of treating it as a blocker?
4. How would you measure whether AI security is improving?

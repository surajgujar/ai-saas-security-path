# Q4 — Leading an AI Security Program (Jul – Sep 2027)

**Why this quarter exists:** "Enabling and protecting AI" is the #1 CISO priority (1,600+ CISOs, 2026). Leadership postings (Head of AI Security Architecture, Leadership TPM AI Security & Compliance, Principal PM AI Model Security) are judged on strategy, metrics, influence and regulatory readiness. The **EU AI Act high-risk deadline (2 Dec 2027)** falls right after this quarter, so EU employers are hiring for it now.
**Outcome:** A complete, presentable **AI Security Program** portfolio, **AAISM** passed, and interviews/offers for Head/Lead/Principal roles abroad.

## Month 1 — Strategy, operating model, metrics (Jul 2027)
- [ ] Write an **AI Security Strategy (3-year)**: vision, principles, capabilities (inventory, guardrails, agent identity, supply chain, detection, governance), operating model (central vs BU vs platform), RACI
- [ ] **Metrics & board reporting**: KPIs/KRIs (% AI systems inventoried and assessed, agents with owned identity, standing-privilege reduction, guardrail ASR trend, mean time to revoke agent credentials); one-page board slide
- [ ] **Budget & business case**: build vs buy (Google Agent Gateway / Model Armor / Wiz AI-APP vs Palo Alto Prisma AIRS vs others), headcount plan, 3-year TCO
- [ ] ISACA **AAISM** study (AI governance & program management, AI risk management, AI technologies & controls)

## Month 2 — Governance & regulation for EU employers (Aug 2027)
- [ ] **EU AI Act** high-risk readiness (Annex III from 2 Dec 2027): risk management, logging, human oversight, accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity; provider vs deployer duties; Art. 50 transparency (live since Aug 2026). Check the latest Official Journal text
- [ ] ISO/IEC 42001 + NIST AI RMF / AI 600-1 + CSA AICM crosswalk; **automate evidence collection** from your Q1–Q3 labs (signatures, evals, authz tests, logs)
- [ ] Cyber Resilience Act touchpoints for AI-enabled products (relevant for Siemens-type manufacturers)
- [ ] **AAISM exam** ✅

## Month 3 — Leadership presence & landing the role (Sep 2027)
- [ ] Final portfolio site: flagship OSS, reference architecture, hardening guide, strategy, board slide, talks
- [ ] Leadership interview prep: "first 90 days as Head of AI Security", conflict with product teams, building a team from zero, hiring bar, managing AI-augmented teams
- [ ] Mock panels: 3 sessions (ask senior contacts or use a coach)
- [ ] Relocation: target 10+ applications/month; prioritise UK (Global Talent evidence pack if eligible), Ireland, Germany; lab and vendor India offices as a transfer route
- [ ] 12-month retrospective + next-year plan (people-management goal: first direct reports)

## Done when
- [ ] Strategy, metrics, business case published (sanitised) · [ ] AAISM passed · [ ] ≥3 final-round interviews abroad

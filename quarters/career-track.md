# Career Track: Leadership, Visibility, Certifications, Relocation (Sep 2026 – Sep 2027)

Runs alongside the technical plan: the "Lead & be visible" (2.5 h) and "Career" (1.5 h) blocks each week.

## Month Q1 — Reposition (Oct – Dec 2026)
- [ ] **Rewrite resume** for Head/Lead of AI Security: add your **people management (team size, hiring, performance reviews, budget)** to the relevant roles; lead with outcomes and metrics; one-line positioning "AI & agent security leader…"
- [ ] Rebuild LinkedIn: headline, About, Featured (this repo), "open to relocation" set to UK/IE/DE/NL/UAE/SG (recruiters only)
- [ ] Start 1 LinkedIn post per week (target 10 this quarter)
- [ ] At work: formally lead an AI security initiative or working group; start mentoring 1–2 people and log it
- [ ] Draft 10 STAR stories, including 4 **people-leadership** stories (hiring, underperformer, conflict, growing someone)
- [ ] Ask your employer about sponsorship for CISSP and AAISM; book CISSP for March
- [ ] Start CISSP study in December (Official Study Guide + practice tests; video course during commute)
- [ ] Build a target list: 40 employers across FS, tech/AI and healthcare in UK/IE/DE/NL/UAE/SG; set job alerts for the §7 titles in PLAN.md

## Month Q2 — Open the market (Jan – Mar 2027)
- [ ] 30+ targeted applications (UK/IE/DE/NL first); 15 recruiter/hiring-manager conversations
- [ ] Reach out to 3 specialist security executive recruiters in London/Dublin/Dubai/Singapore
- [ ] Talk #1 (local OWASP / null / BSides, or virtual)
- [ ] Standards contribution (CoSAI / OWASP GenAI / IETF WIMSE comment)
- [ ] **CISSP exam passed** (by 31 Mar)
- [ ] Ask 3 senior people for recommendation letters (reusable for UK Global Talent)
- [ ] Monthly long-form article (×3)

## Month Q3 — Convert (Apr – Jun 2027)
- [ ] Final rounds: prepare a **30-60-90 plan** and an **AI security strategy on a page** for each final-round company
- [ ] Mock leadership panels ×3 (strategy, stakeholder conflict, team building, budget)
- [ ] Widen search to UAE and Singapore (Head titles), keep UK/EU priority
- [ ] Decide on a UK **Global Talent** application if the evidence pack is strong (talks, OSS, standards, letters)
- [ ] Talk #2 (aim for an international CFP)
- [ ] **ISACA AAISM exam passed** (by 30 Jun)
- [ ] Salary and offer negotiation prep: country cost-of-living benchmark, relocation package asks

## Month Q4 — Land & move (Jul – Sep 2027)
- [ ] Accept offer; start visa process with employer's immigration adviser
- [ ] Hand over at current employer cleanly; secure references
- [ ] Written first-90-days plan for the new role (listen → baseline → quick wins → strategy)
- [ ] Talk #3 or panel; keep weekly posts going (now as a Head/Lead abroad)
- [ ] 12-month retrospective and next-year plan (team scale, Director/CISO-track goals)

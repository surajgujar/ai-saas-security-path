# 30-60-90 Day Pitch (draft — refine by Week 12)

## First 30 days — Listen & map
- Meet IT, Product, AI/Data Science, IAM, SOC, GRC leads; understand current AI & SaaS security ownership
- Inventory: AI systems in production/pilot, agent frameworks in use, top SaaS apps by data sensitivity, current SSPM/CASB coverage
- Review existing standards against OWASP LLM/Agentic Top 10, ATLAS, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act
- Quick win: assess one high-visibility AI initiative end to end

## 60 days — Baseline & quick wins
- Publish v1 AI system intake & risk-assessment process
- Draft reference guardrail + agent identity pattern with IAM (NHI ownership, short-lived creds, runtime authz)
- SSPM: baseline top-10 SaaS apps; OAuth third-party app review
- Shadow AI discovery report

## 90 days — Strategy & scale
- AI & SaaS security roadmap (12 months) agreed with stakeholders
- Continuous AI red-teaming pattern in CI/CD piloted with one team
- Detection content for agent & SaaS abuse handed to SOC
- Mentoring: internal AI security enablement sessions for security colleagues
- KPIs defined and first dashboard live
